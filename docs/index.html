Semiconductor · US
29 tickers — NVDA, TSMC, ASML, AMD, Intel, Qualcomm and their supply chain. Runs every 15 min. Cascade reasoning enabled: a signal on NVDA can fire secondaries on TSMC, ASML, and competitors.
BMV · Mexico
Mexican stock exchange tickers analyzed in Spanish. Runs every 30 min. Independent pipeline — no cross-sector cascade since BMV spans unrelated industries.
Capabilities

Features

🧠
DeepSeek Sentiment Analysis
Every article is analyzed via function calling on deepseek-chat. High-conviction articles escalate to deepseek-reasoner (R1) for chain-of-thought analysis.
🔗
Supply Chain Cascade
A directed graph of supplier/customer/competitor relationships. A primary signal on one ticker automatically fires secondary signals on impacted companies across the chain.
🔄
Learning Loop
Weekly logistic regression on graded signal outcomes. Fitted factor importances are normalized into suggested weights and applied as a per-universe overlay — the model learns from its own calls.
💬
Telegram Chat Advisor
Free-text messages in Telegram are answered by DeepSeek-R1 with access to your live portfolio snapshot. Conversation history per chat, with a system prompt that keeps it on-topic.
💼
Paper Trading Portfolio
Conviction-weighted position sizing with mark-to-market P&L. Tracks signal outcomes at 1d, 7d, and 90d horizons to measure alpha decay and feed the calibration loop.
⚙️
Celery Task Scheduler
Beat schedule drives both pipelines independently — 15-min intraday for semiconductors, 30-min for BMV, daily summaries, and weekly weight recalibration. Deployed with Docker Compose + Redis.
